# Public Meeting Data Validation & Visualization

A data operations and analytics project focused on improving the accuracy, reliability, and usability of city council and public-sector meeting data.

---

## Project Overview
This project involved discovering official government meeting URLs, validating an existing dataset, and visualising link availability across departments and platforms to support reliable public-data workflows.

---

## What I Did

### 1. URL Discovery
- Identified official meeting pages for:
  - City Councils  
  - Planning Departments  
  - Zoning Boards  
- Used verified platforms such as:
  - YouTube  
  - Legistar  
  - Granicus  
  - Swagit  
  - AgendaCenter  
- Documented discovery methods (search, site navigation, platform lookup).

### 2. Data Validation
- Reviewed each entry and classified links as:
  - Valid  
  - Broken  
  - Replaced  
  - Duplicate  
  - Iframe-based  
- Replaced outdated links using official municipal sources.  
- Corrected mislabeled departments and duplicate entries.

### 3. Data Visualization
Created three core analyses:
1. Department-wise link availability  
2. Planning department coverage vs other departments  
3. Platform-wise distribution of meeting links

---

## Key Insights
- Planning department meetings show **lower digital coverage** compared to other departments.  
- **YouTube, Granicus, and Legistar** are the most commonly used hosting platforms.  
- Cleaning outdated and duplicate links significantly improves data reliability for automation and scraping workflows.
